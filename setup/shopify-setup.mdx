CleanClicks integrates with Shopify through three components: a **GA4 first-party loader** (code in your theme), a **Custom Pixel** (ecommerce events), and an **order webhook** (server-side order backup). Which components you use depends on your plan.

All three are set up from the guided Shopify setup in your CleanClicks dashboard, under **Configuration** → **Ecommerce** tab, in the **Shopify** card. The guided setup has four cards: **1 · Your Shopify store**, **2 · Add the CleanClicks pixel**, **3 · Add the order webhook**, and **4 · GA4 first-party loader** (optional). It opens each Shopify admin page for you. Nothing is installed in your store and nothing is changed for you.

| Component | Plan Required | What It Does |
|---|---|---|
| GA4 First-Party Analytics | Signal or Clarity | Routes GA4 through your own domain to keep the sessions ad blockers hide |
| Custom Pixel | Signal or Clarity | Tracks 7 ecommerce events in the pixel sandbox |
| Server-Side Webhook | Signal or Clarity | Captures orders directly from Shopify servers |

## Prerequisites

- Your domain must be added and verified in CleanClicks (CNAME active)
- You need admin access to your Shopify store
- For GA4 proxy: a Google Analytics 4 property

> **Heads up for anyone editing theme code:** Shopify's theme code editor uses a Monaco-based editor that can break silently under strict privacy settings. Firefox with ad-blockers, Enhanced Tracking Protection in "Strict" mode, or similar privacy extensions can prevent the editor from loading, loading save buttons, or rendering the file tree. If the editor looks broken or unresponsive, try Chrome or Edge, or temporarily allow `shopify.com` and `cdn.shopify.com` in your privacy extensions. The same applies to installing the Custom Pixel in Step 2.

## Step 1: GA4 First-Party Analytics (Signal or Clarity)

This step is optional. The GA4 first-party loader routes GA4 traffic through your own first-party domain to keep the sessions ad blockers hide. It fires the storefront ecommerce events to GA4: `view_item`, `view_item_list`, `add_to_cart`, `begin_checkout`, and `search`. Depending on your theme, it may not send `add_to_cart` or `begin_checkout`.

### Get the Loader Code in CleanClicks

1. In your CleanClicks dashboard, go to **Configuration** → **Ecommerce** tab. The guided Shopify setup is in the **Shopify** card.
2. Open card **4 · GA4 first-party loader**. It opens after you click **Save secret** on card 3. If your setup is already done, the card shows the **Shopify setup** summary instead: click **Set up** on its **GA4 first-party loader** row.
3. Click **Copy code and open Themes**. This copies the loader code and opens **Themes** in your Shopify admin. You can also click **Copy** on the code box.

If the card says **Connect GA4** instead of showing code, the loader does not have a GA4 measurement ID yet. Connect GA4 on the **Connections** page, then come back to the card. If the card still asks for GA4 after you connect it, contact support.

### Install the Loader in Your Theme

1. In Themes, on your live theme, click the three dots, then **Edit code**
2. Open `layout/theme.liquid`
3. Paste the code just above the closing `</head>` tag
4. Click **Save**
5. Back on card 4, click **Check my storefront**. When the code is live, the card shows **Loader found on your storefront**.

### Disable Existing GA4

Remove or comment out any existing GA4/gtag.js scripts to avoid duplicate tracking. CleanClicks loads its own proxied copy of gtag.js, and two copies send every event twice. Three places to check:

- **Theme script:** remove or comment out any `gtag.js` or `googletagmanager.com/gtag/js` script tags in your theme
- **Google & YouTube app:** open the app's settings and turn off its GA4 tracking
- **Google Tag Manager:** if you run GA4 through GTM, remove the GA4 tags from your container

Note which property and web stream each one used before you remove it, so you have a rollback path.

### Configure API Secret for Purchase Events

The Custom Pixel (Step 2) sends GA4 purchase events through the Measurement Protocol. This requires an API Secret:

1. In your CleanClicks dashboard, go to **Configuration** → **Vendors** tab and find the **Google Analytics 4** card
2. Enter your GA4 **API Secret** (found in GA4 Admin > Data Streams > your stream > Measurement Protocol API secrets) and click **Save Google Analytics 4**
3. Leave the API Secret saved. The Shopify pixel generator reads it, and purchase events reach GA4 through it. Installing the pixel is not on its own a reason to change the GA4 server-side setting. If GA4 starts showing duplicate purchases, contact support instead of flipping that setting yourself. See [GA4 Connections](/connections/ga4).

### Google Signals / Demographics

GA4 demographics reports (age, gender, interests) and GA4 remarketing audiences need Google Signals. The option is **Turn on Google Signals**. It appears in the GA4 step of setup when CleanClicks creates a new GA4 property for you, and it is off by default.

Our recommendation is to leave it off. Demographics only come from visitors who are signed into Google with Ads Personalization on, which is a slice of your traffic rather than a census, so the picture is often inaccurate. It will not change your Google Ads results either, because CleanClicks sends conversions to Google Ads directly. Turn it on and Google may also hide report rows for small audience segments, which hits smaller-traffic sites hardest.

## Step 2: Install the Custom Pixel (Signal or Clarity)

The Custom Pixel is generated for your domain with your configuration baked in. It runs in Shopify's sandboxed iframe and tracks the full ecommerce funnel. When GA4 is configured, it also sends the GA4 purchase event on checkout completion.

1. In your CleanClicks dashboard, go to **Configuration** → **Ecommerce** tab. In the **Shopify** card, enter your store address on card **1 · Your Shopify store** and click **Continue**. If the card shows the **Shopify setup** summary instead, click **Redo setup**.
2. On card **2 · Add the CleanClicks pixel**, click **Copy code and open Customer events**. This copies your pixel code (generated for your domain) and opens Customer events in your Shopify admin. You can also click **Copy** on the code box.
3. In Shopify Admin, go to **Settings** > **Customer events**
4. Click **Add custom pixel**
5. Name it **CleanClicks**
6. In the **Customer privacy** section, set Permission to **Not required**
7. Set Data sale to **Does not qualify as data sale**
8. If Shopify shows a Data collection setting, set it to **Collected from this website or app**
9. Paste the pixel code into the code editor
10. Click **Save**, then click **Connect**

> **Important:** You must click both **Save** and **Connect**. A saved but unconnected pixel won't fire.

> **Why "Not required"?** The CleanClicks pixel runs for every visitor and applies the visitor's consent choice itself. When a visitor declines, it sends events as measurement-only and removes their hashed email. See [CCPA and opt-out behavior](/reference/data-privacy#ccpa-and-opt-out-behavior) for what each platform receives.

> **Note:** If you've already installed an older version of the pixel, delete it and create a fresh one. Always use the latest code from the dashboard.

### What the Pixel Tracks

| Event | Trigger |
|---|---|
| `page_view` | Every page load |
| `view_item` | Product page view |
| `view_item_list` | Collection page view |
| `add_to_cart` | Item added to cart |
| `begin_checkout` | Checkout started |
| `purchase` | Order completed (also sends GA4 MP if configured) |
| `search` | Search performed |

## Step 3: Server-Side Webhook (Signal or Clarity)

The webhook provides a server-side order path. Verify webhook delivery, event and order IDs, deduplication, and the intended destination receipt before treating the purchase path as accepted.

1. In the guided Shopify setup, click **Continue to the webhook** on card 2 (or **Skip for now**) to open card **3 · Add the order webhook**
2. Click **Copy URL and open Webhooks**. This copies the **Webhook URL** and opens **Settings** > **Notifications** > **Webhooks** in your Shopify admin.
3. Click **Create webhook**
4. Event: **Order payment**
5. Format: **JSON**
6. URL: paste the webhook URL
7. Set **Webhook API version** to the newest dated version marked **(Latest)**. The dropdown defaults to **unstable**. Do not use unstable or Release candidate.
8. Click **Save**
9. Scroll to the bottom of the Webhooks page. Under the list, Shopify shows one line: "All your webhooks will be signed with" followed by a long code. That code is the signing secret. Copy it.
10. Back on card 3, paste it under **Paste the signing secret from Shopify** and click **Save secret**. CleanClicks stores it encrypted and never shows it again.

> **Important:** Do not use Shopify's **Send test** button. It would post a fake order into your ad platforms.

> **Refunds and cancellations:** to reverse refunded value in your ad platforms, also subscribe Shopify's **Refund created** and **Order cancellation** events to the same webhook URL, then map the `refund` (and `cancellation`) event for each platform in **Configuration → Vendors**. Without that mapping the reversal is recorded but not uploaded, so a refund is never sent as a purchase.

## Testing

After setup, verify each component:

### GA4 First-Party Loader
1. On card 4, click **Check my storefront** and wait for **Loader found on your storefront**
2. Visit your store in an Incognito window
3. Open GA4 **Realtime** report
4. Browse a product page — look for `view_item`

### Custom Pixel
1. On card 2, click **Open your storefront** and load any page. The card shows **Pixel verified** when the first event arrives.
2. Browse products, add to cart, search
3. Complete a test checkout. The order appears under **Recent conversions** on the **Dashboard**.

### Webhook
1. Complete a real paid test order (not Shopify's **Send test**)
2. In the **Shopify setup** summary on the **Ecommerce** tab, click **Re-check**. The **Webhook** row reads verified after CleanClicks processes the first paid order. If it says the order had no customer email or could not be fully processed, a new paid order is needed to check again.
3. Under **Recent conversions** on the **Dashboard**, the order row shows one light per destination: GAds, Meta, TikTok, MS, and GA4

## Testing with a Shopify Dev Store (optional)

If you want to test CleanClicks on a sandbox store before installing on your live store, use a Shopify dev store. Two gotchas that cost real time:

**Use the Dev Dashboard, not the Partner Dashboard.** Shopify deprecated Partner Dashboard store creation during the 2025/2026 Dev Dashboard migration. If you click "Add store" in your old Partner Dashboard you'll create a dev store that has restrictions you can't lift later. Create stores at [dev.shopify.com](https://dev.shopify.com) instead.

**Pick the right dev store tier when you create it.** The Dev Dashboard offers multiple dev store variants. The default "Basic App Development" tier strips theme editing — the Online Store channel looks installed but the theme editor returns "This feature is unavailable on your plan." You cannot upgrade or transfer a Basic App Development store to lift this, and Shopify support will confirm. Pick a tier that includes full theme editing (the "Advanced"-equivalent option in the current Dev Dashboard flow) before you do anything else. If you're already stuck on a Basic store, create a new one at the right tier — the old store is unrecoverable for theme-based testing.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|---|---|
| GA4 events not appearing | Verify the loader code is before `</head>` in `layout/theme.liquid`. Click **Check my storefront** on card 4. Check that no other GA4 script is running. |
| Pixel not firing | Make sure you clicked both **Save** and **Connect** in Shopify Customer events |
| Purchase not in GA4 | Check that the API Secret is saved on the **Google Analytics 4** card of the **Vendors** tab |
| Duplicate GA4 events | Remove your existing GA4 setup (see "Disable Existing GA4" above) |
| Webhook row says Shopify sent an order CleanClicks could not verify | Re-copy the signing secret from Shopify and save it again on card 3 |
