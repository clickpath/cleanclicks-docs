CleanClicks currently offers Signal and Clarity through public self-service signup.

## Current Plans

| Limit | Signal | Clarity |
|---|---:|---:|
| Monthly price | $399 | $899 |
| Domains | 1 | Unlimited |
| Monthly sessions | 50,000 | 250,000 |
| Inbound integration keys per domain | 5 | 10 |
| Current ad destinations | Any 3 of 4 | All 4 |
| GA4 and Usermaven analytics layer | Included | Included |

The four current ad destinations are Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, and Microsoft Ads. GA4 and Usermaven are analytics connections and do not use an ad-destination slot.

See the current [pricing page](https://cleanclicks.ai/pricing/) for the checkout path and public offer details.

## Every Plan Includes

- CCPA opt-out support, honoring both a Global Privacy Control browser signal and the CleanClicks opt-out cookie
- 90-day storage of failed vendor uploads

## Choosing

Choose Signal for one domain using no more than three current ad destinations and no more than 50,000 monthly sessions.

Choose Clarity for more than one domain, all four current ad destinations, up to 10 inbound integration keys per domain, or up to 250,000 monthly sessions. Clarity also unlocks the **GA4 accuracy evidence** view, which compares your settled GA4 counts against what CleanClicks recorded for the same window.

Reaching your monthly session allowance does not stop tracking. Collection and delivery continue past the allowance. Session usage appears on the signed-in **Billing** page, monthly is the only billing interval you can select today, and support can confirm your overage terms.

## Existing Accounts

An older or internally provisioned account may display a legacy plan name. Pulse is not available through current public signup. Command is not a self-service public tier. The plan and terms shown in the signed-in Billing page control an existing account. Contact support if the account label or limits do not match the agreement.

## Money-Back Guarantee

The first charge on your first subscription is covered by our **14-day money-back guarantee**. If you are not satisfied for any reason, request a full refund at [helpdesk@cleanclicks.io](mailto:helpdesk@cleanclicks.io) within 14 days of that charge. The guarantee applies once per customer. It includes a first annual prepayment where annual billing applies, but excludes renewals and overage fees. Refunds return to the original payment method. See our [Terms of Service](https://cleanclicks.ai/terms/).
