CleanClicks currently offers Signal and Clarity through public self-service signup.

## Current Plans

| Limit | Signal | Clarity |
|---|---:|---:|
| Monthly price | $399 | $899 |
| Domains | 1 | Unlimited |
| Monthly sessions | 50,000 | 250,000 |
| Current ad destinations | Any 3 of 4 | All 4 |
| GA4 and Usermaven analytics layer | Included | Included |

The four current ad destinations are Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, and Microsoft Ads. GA4 and Usermaven are analytics connections and do not use an ad-destination slot.

See the current [pricing page](https://cleanclicks.ai/pricing/) for the checkout path and public offer details.

## Choosing

Choose Signal for one domain using no more than three current ad destinations and no more than 50,000 monthly sessions.

Choose Clarity for more than one domain, all four current ad destinations, Clarity-only analytics or monitoring features, or up to 250,000 monthly sessions.

Review the signed-in **Billing** page before relying on annual billing, overage charges, warning emails, or continued collection after a monthly allowance is reached.

## Existing Accounts

An older or internally provisioned account may display a legacy plan name. Pulse is not available through current public signup. Command is not a self-service public tier. The plan and terms shown in the signed-in Billing page control an existing account. Contact support if the account label or limits do not match the agreement.

## Money-Back Guarantee

All plans are billed at checkout and backed by a **14-day money-back guarantee** — if you're not satisfied for any reason, contact support within 14 days of your first charge for a full refund.
