Manage your subscription, view invoices, and change plans from the Billing page.

## Accessing Billing

Go to **Billing** in the sidebar.

## Your Subscription

The Billing page shows:

- **Current plan** — Signal or Clarity for current public accounts; an older or internally provisioned account may show a legacy label
- **Billing cycle** — the interval currently attached to the subscription
- **Current period** — when the billing period started and when it renews
- **Status** — active, past due, or canceled; an account created before checkout finishes may briefly show trialing

The signed-in Billing page and current account terms control. Contact support if the displayed plan or terms do not match the agreement.

## Choosing a Plan

To subscribe:

1. Go to **Billing**.
2. Review the current Signal and Clarity plan cards.
3. Select the plan that fits your domain, session, and current ad-destination needs.
4. Complete checkout through Stripe.

Stripe handles payment processing. CleanClicks does not store your credit card details.

## Changing Plans

Open the signed-in **Billing** page and use only the plan and billing controls currently shown there. Timing, proration, and available billing intervals depend on the current subscription and accepted account terms. Review the Stripe portal result before treating a change as complete.

## Invoices

View and download past invoices from the Billing page. Invoices are generated by Stripe and include:

- Plan charges
- Any usage charge shown on the accepted invoice

## Managing Payment

Click **Manage Billing** to open the Stripe billing portal where you can:

- Update payment method
- Change billing address
- View full invoice history
- Cancel subscription

## Money-Back Guarantee

Every subscription is billed at checkout and backed by a **14-day money-back guarantee**:

- Full refund of your first charge, for any reason
- Just contact support within 14 days of the charge
- No hoops — the refund is processed by our team, usually within one business day

## Overages

Do not assume a named overage rate, warning email, or uninterrupted collection after the monthly allowance is reached. Review the signed-in Billing page and current account terms.

| Current plan | Monthly session allowance | Next step when the allowance is approached |
|---|---:|---|
| Signal | 50,000 | Review usage and decide whether Clarity fits |
| Clarity | 250,000 | Contact support before relying on an overage assumption |

## Cancellation

To cancel your subscription:

1. Go to **Billing**
2. Click **Manage Billing** to open the Stripe portal
3. Click **Cancel subscription**

Your account remains active until the end of the current billing period. After that, tracking stops and your configuration is retained for 90 days in case you resubscribe.
