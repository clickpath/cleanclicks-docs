CleanClicks currently offers two self-service plans. Signal is $399 per month for one domain, up to 50,000 monthly sessions, and any three of the four currently offered ad destinations. Clarity is $899 per month for unlimited domains, up to 250,000 monthly sessions, and all four currently offered ad destinations.

The current destination set is Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, and Microsoft Ads. GA4 and Usermaven are analytics connections and do not use an ad-destination slot.

## Plans at a Glance

| Limit | Signal | Clarity |
|---|---:|---:|
| Monthly price | $399 | $899 |
| Domains | 1 | Unlimited |
| Monthly sessions | 50,000 | 250,000 |
| Inbound integration keys per domain | 5 | 10 |
| Current ad destinations | Any 3 of 4 | All 4 |
| GA4 and Usermaven analytics layer | Included | Included |

## Which Plan Fits?

### Signal

Signal fits one domain using no more than three current ad destinations and no more than 50,000 monthly sessions.

### Clarity

Clarity fits more than one domain, all four current ad destinations, up to 10 inbound integration keys per domain, or up to 250,000 monthly sessions. Clarity also unlocks the **GA4 accuracy evidence** view, which compares your settled GA4 counts against what CleanClicks recorded for the same window.

If you are not ready to choose, start with the free, directional [Tracking Health Check](https://cleanclicks.ai/tracking-health-check/). If you need help choosing or configuring a route, [book a walkthrough](https://cleanclicks.ai/book/).

See the current [pricing page](https://cleanclicks.ai/pricing/) for the checkout path and public offer details.

## Usage and Billing Details

Reaching your monthly session allowance does not stop tracking. CleanClicks keeps collecting and keeps delivering events to your connected destinations past the allowance.

Your current session usage appears on the signed-in **Billing** page. Monthly is the only billing interval you can select today. For overage terms, contact [helpdesk@cleanclicks.io](mailto:helpdesk@cleanclicks.io).

## Existing Accounts

An older or internally provisioned account may display a legacy plan name. Pulse is not available through current public signup. Command is not a self-service public tier. The plan and terms shown in the signed-in Billing page control an existing account. Contact support if the account label or limits do not match the agreement.

## Money-Back Guarantee

The first charge on your first subscription is covered by our **14-day money-back guarantee**. If you are not satisfied for any reason, request a full refund at [helpdesk@cleanclicks.io](mailto:helpdesk@cleanclicks.io) within 14 days of that charge. The guarantee applies once per customer. It includes a first annual prepayment where annual billing applies, but excludes renewals and overage fees. Refunds return to the original payment method. See our [Terms of Service](https://cleanclicks.ai/terms/).
